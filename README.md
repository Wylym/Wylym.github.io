# Chronicle

A life simulator you can play offline on your phone. Live a whole life, one year at a time — school, love, family, careers, the military academy, emigration, social media and more. English, French, Russian and Spanish.

**Play:** https://wylym.github.io/chronicle/

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open Chronicle from your home screen. After the first launch it works offline.

Saves stay on your phone. Use **Settings → Back up all lives** to keep a backup file.

Version `bc3febe3fb`.
